Digital World Cosmology

Cosmology atlas

How the Digital Worlds fit together: three worlds with their own host computers and guardians, the Kernel watched by angels, and the underworlds that sink toward one shared abyss. This atlas is an editorial model built from official Bandai material, not a universal chain of command; the labels below mark how firmly each link is established.

Overseer, in some continuities

Homeostasis

Continuity-SpecificUnconfirmed

A formless entity tied to the balance of the Digital World. In Adventure it chose the DigiDestined; according to the wikis, Digimon Story: Time Stranger makes it the being above all three host computers. No official profile describes it, and no anime series places it over Iliad or Shambala, so the atlas does not draw it as the ruler of every world.

Special structure

Kernel

Established LoreScope unconfirmed

God's Domain, rumored to compute all the data of the Digital World and guarded by the Three Great Angels, with the Angelic Army beneath them. Its reach beyond the Classic world is not established.

Shared sea

Net Ocean

Established Lore Links every world: unconfirmed

The great ocean of the network where Digimon were born. Shambala's continent and islands float on it, and AncientMermaidmon is its guardian goddess. The atlas draws the three worlds inside it; official material never describes it as a conscious or governing system.

Shared deepest layer

Cocytus

Established LoreNo ruler named

A single lowest layer connected from each Dark Area and common to every Digital World. The Seven Great Demon Lords, GranDracmon and Gulfmon make it their stronghold as separate powers; no source names anyone its ruler.

Reading the atlas

the rules this model follows

Many Memberships
A Digimon can belong to several groups at once: Azulongmon is both a Sovereign and a Great Dragon, and Enmamon is one of the Tentei Hachibushu as well as the ruler of Yomi.
History Is Not Membership
A tie to the past is not a place in the present: the Ten Legendary Warriors and the Titans belong to ancient history, not to today's governments.
Shared Myths, Not Rivalries
Similar inspiration does not make enemies: the Four Sovereigns and the Shikyoju mirror each other in theme only.
Not Every Group Reports Up
An organization does not necessarily answer to its world's host computer: no source places the Kernel's angels or the Four Sovereigns under Yggdrasill.
Living There Is Not Ruling
GranDracmon and Gulfmon make Cocytus their stronghold without governing it, and the Seven Great Demon Lords never act as one government.
Absence Is Not Proof
No Kernel has been described for Iliad or Shambala, and no Deva for Iliad. A structure missing from one world's sources may still exist there.
Continuities Stay Separate
Each anime, manga and game keeps its own timeline. When a fact belongs to one of them, it carries the Continuity-Specific label instead of joining one merged history.
A Model, Not a Chain of Command
The atlas arranges official material so it can be read in one place. Its layout is editorial; it is not an official hierarchy of the Digital Worlds.
