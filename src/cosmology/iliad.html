Digital World: Iliad

A multi-layered Digital World run by the host computer Homeros, where Greek and Roman mythology runs deep in the data. Twelve guardian gods watch over its areas in pairs, while the ruler of the dead keeps the lowest layer apart from them.

A world of layers

temples, cities, seas and forges stacked above an underworld

Iliad is a Digital World managed by the host computer Homeros, and it has evolved in its own way, unlike other Digital Worlds; official material infers that data from Greek and Roman mythology is deeply involved. It is a multi-layered world of independent areas with very different environments, separated by a void and linked by rails. In ancient times it was a single huge continent, until the continent collapsed and split into pieces.

Homeros usually keeps silent and rarely takes part in running the world: it is programmed to act when Iliad faces a crisis that threatens its survival. It has no fixed form, and the guardians revere it through the symbol of a lyre.

Established Lore A 2014 Premium Bandai product text (Digimon Adventure Digivice ver.15th) places Iliad on a different server from the Digital World that King Drasil governs. The Royal Knights have not interfered there because the Olympos XII protect it, though King Drasil's will or a virus spreading across the world may one day bring them face to face.

Olympos XII

main guardian organization: twelve gods under Homeros

Olympos XII

12 membersEstablished Lore

Twelve guardians gathered under Homeros, with Jupitermon at their head, protect Iliad pair by pair (see the regions above). Marsmon's profile credits them with abilities rivaling the Royal Knights while staying neutral in the incidents of the Digital World. In ancient times they defeated the Titans. Plutomon acts apart from them and has a longstanding feud with Jupitermon.

Titans

historical faction: the people of Erebus

Titans

No fixed rosterLegendary

The collective name for the Digimon who lived in Erebus, a dark city that developed its own civilization inside Iliad's Dark Area. Membership was not a matter of species: outsiders could join if the Titans accepted them. In ancient Iliad they invaded the other areas and fought a fierce war against the Twelve; they were defeated and nearly annihilated, and why they invaded is still unknown. Plutomon keeps their souls in Erebus, now a ruin full of vengeful spirits.

Established Lore

The Dark Area is the underworld where the souls of dead Digimon go, the lowest region of multi-layered Iliad. Plutomon reigns there as ruler, acting separately from the Twelve: it is not a member of the Olympos XII.

The realm returns properly functioning DigiCores to the upper areas as Digi-Eggs and imprisons DigiCores that carry serious bugs, so death and rebirth both pass through it. Plutomon breaks that cycle for one people: it keeps the souls of the defeated Titans in Erebus instead of returning them.

Plutomon shares Jupitermon's goal of punishing evil, but the two have a longstanding animosity, because Jupitermon does not approve of Plutomon's cruelty. Compare it with the other underworld authorities on Dark Areas & Cocytus.

Celestial Walker and Astral Sentinel

line-ups set in Iliad, not organizations

Digimon Pendulum COLOR 8

Celestial Walker

"One who crosses the heavens." A virtual pet version set in Iliad, released in Olympos Green. Its Olympos XII are Apollomon, Jupitermon, Vulcanusmon, Bacchusmon, Neptunemon and Merukimon.

Digimon Pendulum COLOR 9

Astral Sentinel

"Guardian of the astral realm." Its companion version in Iliad Blue, with Marsmon, Venusmon, Dianamon, Ceresmon, Junomon and Minervamon, plus Plutomon. Apollomon and Dianamon Jogress across the two into GraceNovamon.

How to read them

Products, not powers

Both names are official, but they belong to two virtual pets that split the Twelve between them. They have no leaders or mission inside the story, so the atlas treats them as thematic line-ups of Iliad's Digimon.
