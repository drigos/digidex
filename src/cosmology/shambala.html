The Eastern Digital World

a world of continents and islands, observed only at its center

Official material introduces Shambala as one more world, following the Digital World and Digital World: Iliad. Also called the Eastern Digital World, it is made of a continent and small islands floating in the Net Ocean, and it strongly reflects data from around East Asia in the Real World. Only the area considered the world's center has been observed so far.

Kunlun, its host computer, ranks alongside King Drasil and Homeros, the administrators of the other worlds. It has no fixed form, its symbol resembles a taijitu, and it normally lies dormant: it may intervene only when Shambala falls into a state it can no longer sustain.

Legendary

By order of Kunlun, Susanoomon destroyed the system that had existed since ancient times and, with the power of creation, rewrote it into the Shambala of today. The official page presents this as legend: so the legend records.

Susanoomon is not one of the eight guardians. The Tentei Hachibushu, summoned by Kunlun, protect present-day Shambala in its place.

Continuity-Specific In Frontier, Susanoomon is the form born from all ten Legendary Spirits, shown at the end of all ten Legendary Warrior trees. The Shambala legend is a separate story about the same species.

Four Sovereigns

Classic Digital World. Four holy beasts guarding the compass points, with Huanglongmon at the center: order, protection and balance.

Thematic Parallel

Shikyoju

Shambala. Four ancient perilous beasts held under seal in Jakeizan: ruin, malice and confinement.

Both foursomes draw on Chinese cosmology from opposite sides, which makes them a useful mirror. No official source links the two groups, places them in the same world, or has them fight.

How to read them

Ecosystems, not armies

Officially these are the names of two virtual pet versions, each gathering the Digimon of one region's ecosystem. They have no leaders, ranks or mission, so the atlas treats them as thematic classifications.

Yomi

the underworld of Shambala

Yomi

Ruler: EnmamonEstablished Lore

The official page calls Yomi an underworld equivalent to the Dark Areas of other worlds. It lies underground, has its own ecosystem and many Digimon that live nowhere else, and even the living can apparently cross into it by Yomotsuhirasaka, the slope that continues from Ashino Island. Both names come from the land of the dead in Japanese mythology.

Digimon of the Eastern Digital World are sent to Yomi after death. Enmamon reads their DigiCores: a core found to carry a bug is confined in Yomi for good and never reborn on the surface. The same system binds Enmamon itself, which cannot ascend to the world above.

EnmamonRuler of Yomi, eighth of the Tentei Hachibushu
Unconfirmed Digimon Profile #035 says every Digital World's Dark Area leads down to a single shared layer, Cocytus, but it predates Shambala and never mentions Yomi. Since Yomi plays the role of a Dark Area, a link to Cocytus is plausible, not established. See Dark Areas & Cocytus.
