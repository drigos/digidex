Digital World (Classic)

The Digital World of most anime series, governed by the host computer Yggdrasill. Its knights take their name from medieval chivalry and its host from the world tree of Norse myth, while holy beasts of Chinese cosmology guard its compass and an ancient war still echoes through its history.

The world under Yggdrasill

the ordinary Digital World, as official material calls it

Official material refers to this world simply as the Digital World, or the ordinary Digital World when it sets it beside Iliad and Shambala; the atlas calls it Classic to tell them apart. Yggdrasill, the mysterious host computer, governs the entirety of the Digital World on the Network. The Royal Knights are the highest rank of network security under its jurisdiction: Yggdrasill apparently selects them, and each knight interprets its orders for itself.

The world's identity is mixed. The host computer is named after the world tree of Norse myth and the knights draw on medieval chivalry, but the Four Sovereigns and their Deva come from Chinese and Buddhist cosmology, and the Ten Legendary Warriors belong to an ancient era of their own.

Continuity-Specific Each anime series, film and game shows its own version of this world. The atlas groups them as the Classic world for orientation, without merging their timelines into one history.

Royal Knights

main guardian organization: the thirteen Holy Knights

Royal Knights

13 membersEstablished Lore

Said to number thirteen, the knights are the highest rank of network security in Yggdrasill's Digital World. Because each one interprets Yggdrasill's orders for itself, their ideas of justice do not always align, and some have turned against the world they guard. Alphamon, the Lord of the Empty Seat, stays hidden from normal sight as a deterrent.

Four Sovereigns and the Deva

secondary organization: guardians of the compass and their twelve generals

Four Sovereigns

4 guardians + the CenterEstablished Lore

Four Holy Beasts that have protected the Digital World and governed its stability since the age of myth, each ruling a compass point and commanding three of the twelve Deva. Huanglongmon, the golden emperor of the Center, stands above them as their ruler rather than as a fifth guardian; it was sealed deep underground in ancient times. They are not subordinates of the Royal Knights: no official text ranks one group under the other.

Ten Legendary Warriors

historical group: the saviors of the Ancient Digital World

Ten Legendary Warriors

10 membersLegendary

Ancient warriors, one per element, who saved the Ancient Digital World and are said to be among the first Mega Digimon. AncientGreymon and AncientGarurumon survived the holy war to the end and are said to have sealed Lucemon, and the warriors' power lives on in twenty Legendary Spirits. They belong to the world's distant past, not to its present government: no official text places them under Yggdrasill or the Royal Knights.

Established Lore

Anubismon guards and supervises the Dark Area and acts as the judge of the Digital World. Its profile says it seals Digimon made of evil data away in eternal darkness and lets Digimon made of good data be reborn as Digi-Eggs; Digimon Profile #035 adds that data judged bad keeps wandering the Dark Area.

Anubismon is a guardian and a judge, not a sovereign. Official material gives every Digital World its own Dark Area, and nowhere calls Anubismon the ruler of all of them. Below this Dark Area lies Cocytus, the single deepest layer shared by every world.
