Dark Areas & Cocytus

Every Digital World has its own underworld, each kept by a different authority with a different role. Beneath them lies Cocytus, a single lowest layer shared by every world, where the most dangerous powers of the dark make their stronghold and no one is named its ruler.

No source calls any of the three the sovereign of all underworlds. Their worlds keep separate administrations, and their roles differ: Anubismon judges, Plutomon rules and hunts the wicked even among the living, and Enmamon governs a realm it is bound to itself.

Cocytus

the shared deepest layer

Established Lore

Digimon Profile #035 describes a lowest-layer area, Cocytus, that connects from each Dark Area. It is common to every Digital World, and only one exists. The profile names the ordinary Digital World and Iliad; it predates Shambala and does not mention Yomi.

Cocytus has no named ruler. The Seven Great Demon Lords, GranDracmon and Gulfmon all make it their stronghold, but they are separate powers, not a single government, and the Demon Lords are said to neither cooperate nor turn on each other.

Seven Great Demon Lords

7 membersEstablished Lore

Seven Evil King Digimon, each bearing the Crown of a deadly sin. Profile #035 and the official Digimon Chronicle X story both place their stronghold in Cocytus, the lowest layer of the Dark Area; individual profiles speak of the Dark Area's heart and its deepest parts, and Leviamon sleeps in the Net Ocean near it.

Embodiment of the Seven Sins

Related, not an eighth member

Ogudomon is a Super Demon Lord holding all the sins of the Digital World together with the power to atone for them. Its profile never counts it among the Seven. Its X Antibody form, Ogudomon X, further amplifies the Seven Great Demon Lords' evil thoughts and destructive urges, and its arms and head become blades carrying each of their powers.

OgudomonSum of the sins Ogudomon XX Antibody form
Continuity-Specific In the official Digimon Chronicle X story, the defeated Seven are dragged down to Cocytus, where seven copies of Diablomon X absorb them and fuse into Ogudomon X; Lucemon X meant to destroy the whole Digital World by becoming it. After Jesmon GX cuts it down, the released Seven return to Cocytus.

Established Lore Profile #035 introduces GranDracmon and Gulfmon together: like the Seven Great Demon Lords, both make Cocytus their stronghold, and each is an extremely dangerous being.

GranDracmon is the king of all vampire Digimon. It has long kept its own castle in the Dark Area, and its power is so overwhelming that not even the Seven dare to interfere with it; it is believed to have been involved in the creation of the Dark Area and to know the truth behind the mysterious Death-X-evolution. It is not a Demon Lord.

Gulfmon is a Dark Beast that emerged from the abyss of the Dark Area. Its giant maw is said to connect to that abyss, and it is described as capable of destroying the world in seven days. Neither it nor GranDracmon is called the ruler of Cocytus.
