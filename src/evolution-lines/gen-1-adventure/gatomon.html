Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ Virus / X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Only games link YukimiBotamon to Nyaromon, Angewomon to Junomon and Mastemon, and BlackGatomon to LadyDevimon; the tree keeps these links (the Reference Book only says BlackGatomon digivolves into Dark Angels and Mastemon fuses an Angel with a Dark Angel). Omnimon Merciful Mode is a collective composition (Adventure tri.): Magnadramon plus Omnimon, Phoenixmon, HerculesKabuterimon, Rosemon, Vikemon and Seraphimon. Gatomon has no recorded form for the Digi-Egg of Destiny.
