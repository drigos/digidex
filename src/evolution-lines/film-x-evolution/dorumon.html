Lore notes

the stories behind the unusual arrows
Yggdrasill's Experiment
The Reference Book presumes Dorumon to be an experimental Prototype Digimon with a legendary dragon's life force deep in its DigiCore. In the film, Magnamon reveals it is Yggdrasill's experiment.
The Empty Seat
Alphamon holds the Royal Knights' Empty Seat and stays hidden. In the film, DoruGreymon digivolves into it while dueling Omnimon, revealing itself as the thirteenth Royal Knight.
Ouryumon Becomes the Sword
In Digimon Chronicle, Ouryumon dies shielding Alphamon from Dexmon, and Alphamon turns its data into the Ouryuken sword. In the film, Alphamon takes this form without Ouryumon.
Two Origins of DexDorugoramon
In Digimon Chronicle, Dorugoramon falls to Omnimon X and Death-X-evolves into DexDorugoramon. In the film, Yggdrasill builds it from Dorugamon's copied data, and it lays the DexDoruGreymon eggs.
The Empty Shell
In Digimon Chronicle, the management system pulls Dorumon's life data out of DexDorugoramon, but the empty shell lives on as Dexmon, a program with no DigiCore that even Omnimon X cannot delete.
Raised Again
Separated from DexDorugoramon, Dorumon starts over as a Rookie in Digimon Chronicle, and Kota raises it again, this time through Reptiledramon and Grademon to Alphamon.
Back to Dorumon
In the film, Alphamon Ouryuken finds that Dexmon, made from its own data, is its other half. It impales them both, gives its X-Antibody to Omnimon and merges with Dexmon back into Dorumon.

Card labels show Level (+ X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Only the Digimon Pendulum X V-pet and games join Dodomon, Dorimon and Dorumon; the tree keeps them, since the three share the Doru name and their iron-bead attacks. The Reference Book marks the line's forms as X-Antibody carriers, so it has no X-Digivolution branch. Reptiledramon and Dexmon are DigimonWiki's names for the official Raptordramon and DeathXmon.
