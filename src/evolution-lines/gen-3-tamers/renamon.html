Renamon: evolution tree

Fresh -> In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega -> Ultra hierarchy, with the Sakuyamon Biomerge, the D-Ark's alternate line (Youkomon, Doumon, Kuzuhamon), the X-Antibody forms and the Maid Mode changes.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ Virus / X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Biomerge (Matrix Evolution): Taomon and Rika merge into Sakuyamon in Tamers episode 45, as in the D-Ark V-pet, drawn as a Normal evolution; most episodes merge Renamon straight into it, a jump with no arrow of its own. Youkomon, Doumon and Kuzuhamon are the D-Ark's alternate line. The Reference Book says fox Digimon normally become Kuzuhamon, and only the greatest reach Sakuyamon.
