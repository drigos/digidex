Guardromon: evolution tree

In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega hierarchy, with the D-Power line from Kokuwamon to HiAndromon, the Ghost Game branch from Hagurumon and the X-Antibody forms.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Kazu's Guardromon was once an Andromon that regressed from its injuries (Tamers episode 31), and it regresses to Kapurimon in episode 51; no accepted source shows the Fresh form before Kapurimon. Kokuwamon, Guardromon, Andromon and HiAndromon are the Kokuwamon line of the D-Power V-pet. Only games link Kapurimon to Kokuwamon; the tree keeps the link between the anime's In-Training and the D-Power's Rookie.
