Guardromon: evolution tree

In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega hierarchy, with the D-Power line from Kokuwamon to HiAndromon, the Ghost Game branch from Hagurumon and the X-Antibody forms.

Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ Virus / X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Kazu's Guardromon was once an Andromon that regressed from its injuries (Tamers episode 31), and it regresses to Kapurimon in episode 51. Kokuwamon, Guardromon, Andromon and HiAndromon are the Kokuwamon line of the D-Power V-pet.
