Guilmon: evolution tree

Fresh -> In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega -> Ultra hierarchy, with the Gallantmon Biomerge, the D-Ark's dark line (BlackGrowlmon, BlackWarGrowlmon, ChaosGallantmon), the Digital Hazard (Megidramon), the X-Antibody forms, the Data (Yellow) variants and the Crimson Mode change.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book and Wikimon. Card labels show Level (+ Virus / X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Biomerge (Matrix Evolution): Guilmon and Takato merge straight into Gallantmon, drawn as a Normal evolution; the D-Ark V-pet also reaches it from WarGrowlmon. BlackGrowlmon, BlackWarGrowlmon and ChaosGallantmon are the D-Ark's dark line. Grani is an external support unit / steed, not an evolution stage. One theory holds that Megidramon and ChaosGallantmon are the same being seen as either a dark dragon or a dark knight; this is drawn as a faint two-way link, not a path.
