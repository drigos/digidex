Guilmon: evolution tree

Fresh -> In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega -> Ultra hierarchy, with the Gallantmon Biomerge, the D-Ark's dark line (BlackGrowlmon, BlackWarGrowlmon, ChaosGallantmon), the Digital Hazard (Megidramon), the X-Antibody forms, the Data (Yellow) variant line and the Crimson Mode change.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book and Wikimon. Card labels show Level (+ X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Biomerge (Matrix Evolution): WarGrowlmon and Takato merge into Gallantmon in Tamers episode 45, as in the D-Ark V-pet, drawn as a Normal evolution; most episodes merge Guilmon straight into it, a jump with no arrow of its own. BlackGrowlmon, BlackWarGrowlmon and ChaosGallantmon are the D-Ark's dark line. Grani is a Flying Dragon machine that Gallantmon pilots, not an evolution stage. One theory holds that Megidramon and ChaosGallantmon are the same being seen as either a dark dragon or a dark knight; this is drawn as a faint two-way link, not a path.
