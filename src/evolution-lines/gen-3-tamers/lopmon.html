Lopmon: evolution tree

Fresh -> In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega hierarchy, with the Celestial line up to Cherubimon (Good), the dark line from Wendigomon to Cherubimon (Evil), the Frontier corruption and the X-Antibody forms.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. In Tamers, Suzie's Lopmon warp-digivolves into Antylamon (Good), so the scene has no arrow; Turuiemon and the Cherubimon (Good) step come from Adventure: 2020 and from the D-Power V-pet's Lopmon line. The dark line is Willis's Lopmon, infected by a virus, in the Adventure 02 film.
