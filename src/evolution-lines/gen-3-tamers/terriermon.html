Terriermon: evolution tree

Fresh -> In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega hierarchy, with the MegaGargomon Biomerge, the Black Digitron variants, the golden Rapidmon of the Digi-Egg of Destiny and the X-Antibody forms.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Biomerge (Matrix Evolution): Rapidmon and Henry merge into MegaGargomon in Tamers episode 45, as in the D-Ark V-pet, drawn as a Normal evolution; most episodes merge Terriermon straight into it, a jump with no arrow of its own. The golden Rapidmon is the Armor form of Willis's Terriermon in the Adventure 02 film.
