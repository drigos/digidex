Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book and Wikimon. Card labels show Level (+ Virus / X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. In Tamers, Jeri's Leomon never digivolves; Elecmon, GrapLeomon and SaberLeomon are its line in the D-Power V-pet, and the Reference Book puts Punimon and Tsunomon before Elecmon. Adventure's Leomon warp-digivolves into SaberLeomon (episode 47), skipping the Ultimate, so the scene has no arrow. MadLeomon is a Leomon corrupted by the Bagra Army, and it returns to Leomon in Digimon Fusion episode 29. Only games link Leomon to BanchoLeomon; the tree keeps the link, the way BanchoLillymon's profile ties it to Lillymon.
