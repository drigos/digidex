Leomon: evolution tree

Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega hierarchy, with the D-Power line from Elecmon to SaberLeomon, the BanchoLeomon and IceLeomon branches, the Xros Wars MadLeomon and the X-Antibody forms.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book and Wikimon. Card labels show Level (+ Virus / X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. In Tamers, Jeri's Leomon never digivolves; Elecmon, GrapLeomon and SaberLeomon are its line in the D-Power V-pet. MadLeomon is a Leomon corrupted by the Bagra Army, and it returns to Leomon in Xros Wars episode 29. Only games link Leomon to BanchoLeomon; the tree keeps the link, the way BanchoLillymon's profile ties it to Lillymon.
