Cyberdramon: evolution tree

Fresh -> In-Training -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega hierarchy, with the Justimon Biomerge and its three arms, the D-Power line from Monodramon, the Beatbreak branch and the X-Antibody forms.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ Virus / X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. Biomerge (Matrix Evolution): Cyberdramon and Ryo merge into Justimon, drawn as a Normal evolution; the D-Power V-pet reaches Justimon (Accel Arm) from Cyberdramon. Cyberdramon regresses to Monodramon in Tamers episode 41 and to Hopmon in episode 51; Strikedramon comes from the D-Power V-pet, whose Monodramon line runs Monodramon, Strikedramon, Cyberdramon. Justimon swaps between its three arms with the Trinity Arm.
