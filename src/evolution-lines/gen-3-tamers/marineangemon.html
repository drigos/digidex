MarineAngemon: evolution tree

Fresh -> Rookie -> Champion -> Ultimate -> Mega hierarchy, with the D-Power line from Penguinmon through the Net Ocean and the Digimon Next link from Pichimon.
Digivolution triggers
Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show Level (+ X); edge colors show the digivolution trigger. In Tamers, Kenta's MarineAngemon is one of the Mega Digimon that come to help the Four Sovereigns (episode 40) and has no earlier form; Penguinmon, Dolphmon and Whamon are its line in the D-Power V-pet. The In-Training column is left empty: Pichimon becomes MarineAngemon directly in the Digimon Next manga.
