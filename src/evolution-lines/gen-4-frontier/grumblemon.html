Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show the Spirit tier; edge colors show the Spirit trigger. Frontier has no partner Digimon: Cherubimon gave life to the H Spirit of Earth as Grumblemon, who already held the B Spirit to become Gigasmon; AncientVolcanomon is the ancestral Warrior of Earth (lore links, not steps). No Fusion of the Earth Spirits is recorded. In episode 35 Takuya unifies the Spirits of Earth with four other Warriors' Spirits into EmperorGreymon (see panel), which DNA-Digivolves with Koji's MagnaGarurumon into Susanoomon (see panel). Susanoomon and AncientTroiamon are DigimonWiki's names for the official Susanomon and AncientTroymon.
