Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show the Spirit tier; edge colors show the Spirit trigger. Frontier has no partner Digimon: Koichi Spirit-Evolves into these forms and has no Child form; AncientSphinxmon is the ancestral Warrior of Darkness (lore links, not steps). Cherubimon corrupted the Spirits of Darkness into the H and B Spirits, locking Koichi as Duskmon and Velgemon (red); once freed, Koichi purifies them into the L and K Spirits of Loweemon and JagerLoweemon. Rhihimon, the Fusion of the two Darkness Spirits, comes from the Hybrid-Digivolving figures and the D-Tector 3.0 toy, outside the Frontier anime. Koichi gives his Spirits to Koji, who in episode 35 unifies them with four other Warriors' Spirits into MagnaGarurumon (see panel), which DNA-Digivolves with Takuya's EmperorGreymon into Susanoomon (see panel). Velgemon, Susanoomon and AncientMermaidmon are DigimonWiki's names for the official Velgrmon, Susanomon and AncientMermaimon.
