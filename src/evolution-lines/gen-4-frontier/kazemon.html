Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show the Spirit tier; edge colors show the Spirit trigger. Frontier has no partner Digimon: Zoe Spirit-Evolves into these forms and has no Child form; AncientKazemon is the ancestral Warrior of Wind (lore links, not steps). JetSilphymon, the Fusion of the two Wind Spirits, comes from the Reference Book and the D-Tector 3.0 toy, outside the Frontier anime. In episode 35 Takuya unifies the Spirits of Wind with four other Warriors' Spirits into EmperorGreymon (see panel), which DNA-Digivolves with Koji's MagnaGarurumon into Susanoomon (see panel). Susanoomon and AncientTroiamon are DigimonWiki's names for the official Susanomon and AncientTroymon.
