Names link to DigimonWiki/Fandom (Fandom community, CC BY-SA unless noted otherwise); artwork © Bandai, via the official Reference Book. Card labels show the Spirit tier; edge colors show the Spirit trigger. Frontier has no partner Digimon: Cherubimon gave life to the H Spirit of Water as Ranamon, who later found the B Spirit to become Calmaramon; AncientMermaidmon is the ancestral Warrior of Water (lore links, not steps). No Fusion of the Water Spirits is recorded. In episode 35 Koji unifies the Spirits of Water with four other Warriors' Spirits into MagnaGarurumon (see panel), which DNA-Digivolves with Takuya's EmperorGreymon into Susanoomon (see panel). Ranamon, AncientMermaidmon and Susanoomon are DigimonWiki's names for the official Lanamon, AncientMermaimon and Susanomon.
