Frontier has no partner Digimon: Koji's Child form Strabimon Spirit-Evolves into these forms; AncientGarurumon is the ancestral Warrior of Light (lore links, not steps) and the origin of both the Human line (via Strabimon) and the Beast Spirit. BeoWolfmon fuses the two Light Spirits; MagnaGarurumon unifies five Warriors' Spirits (see panel); Susanoomon is MagnaGarurumon DNA-Digivolved with Takuya's EmperorGreymon (see panel), uniting all ten Legendary Warriors.
