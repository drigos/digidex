Celestial Digimon

The three Celestial Digimon that guard the Kernel, the place rumored to compute all the data of the Digital World. Seraphimon carries God's law, Ophanimon God's love and mercy, and Cherubimon guards God and wisdom; together they stand over every other Angel Digimon.

Lore notes

the stories behind the three seats
Guardians of the Kernel
Ophanimon's profile places the trio at the heart of the Digital World, the Kernel (God's Domain). Digimon Profile #015 adds that the Kernel is rumored to compute all the data of the Digital World, and that the world would collapse if it malfunctioned.
Law, Love and Wisdom
Ophanimon's official profile defines the division: Seraphimon is the thorough executor of God's law, Ophanimon the holy mother who conveys love and mercy, and Cherubimon the guardian of God and wisdom.
Lucemon's Divided Power Legendary
Profile #015 and Lucemon's own profile both say, with a hedge, that the power Lucemon once held was split in three and inherited by the Celestial Digimon.
Over Every Angel
Seraphimon governs all angel Digimon. An official digimon.net poll describes the Angelic Army as the Digimon who keep order in the Digital World under the Celestial Digimon, led into battle by SlashAngemon.
When an Angel Falls
Negative emotions that swell day by day can drag one of the three into a fallen form, and falling further is said to make it an Evil King. If one of the three falls, a new Digimon is apparently chosen, evolves and fills the empty seat.
Rasielmon, the Former Peer
Rasielmon once held the same rank as Ophanimon and is said to see all of the Digital World through the Kernel. It is related to the trio's domain but holds none of the three seats.
The Three in Frontier Continuity-Specific
In Frontier, the Ten Legendary Warriors left their Spirits in the care of the three (episode 38), and Cherubimon's fall into darkness sets the series in motion.
