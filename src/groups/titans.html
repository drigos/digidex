Titans

The collective name for the Digimon who lived in Erebus, a dark city that grew its own civilization inside Iliad's Dark Area. In ancient times they invaded the other areas and fought the Olympos XII; they lost and were nearly wiped out, and the reason for the invasion is still unknown.

Established Lore No fixed roster: the official Iliad page says the Titans are not classified by species or type, and that outsiders could join if the Titans accepted them. Each continuity names its own members, so this page highlights only the figures tied to the faction across sources.

Lore notes

the stories behind the fallen city
A City in the Dark
Erebus was a dark city in Iliad's Dark Area where a civilization developed on its own. The official page names its inhabitants, collectively, the Titans.
The Ancient War
The Titans invaded the other areas of ancient Iliad and fought a fierce war against the Olympos XII. They were defeated and almost annihilated. The official page gives the war no name, and no reason for the invasion.
Souls Held in Erebus
Plutomon keeps the souls of the defeated Titans in Erebus instead of returning them to Digi-Eggs. Erebus is now a ruin infested with vengeful spirits.
Titamon's Grudge
Titamon's Reference Book profile never uses the word Titan: it says Titamon was born from the grudge of the Digimon the Olympos XII defeated. The official Digimon Story: Time Stranger site calls it the berserker of the Titans.
Plutomon at the Head Continuity-Specific
The official Time Stranger site introduces Plutomon as the chief of the Titans attacking each region, and Wikimon lists Plutomon as the Titans' leader in Digimon Crusader too. Iliad's own page only makes it the ruler of the Dark Area.
Chronomon Behind the Curtain Continuity-Specific
DigimonWiki and Wikimon describe Chronomon as the true leader of the Titans in Time Stranger, possessing Plutomon and later Junomon. Chronomon's Reference Book profiles mention neither Iliad nor the Titans.
Rosters by Continuity Continuity-Specific
Wikimon lists Plutomon, Titamon, SkullBaluchimon and Orgemon as Titans in Digimon Crusader, while Time Stranger has a much larger cast. None of these lists is the faction's official roster.
