Digital World: Iliad

Olympos XII

The twelve guardians gathered under Homeros, Iliad's host computer, with Jupitermon at their head. Each pair watches over one area of the multi-layered world, and their power is said to rival the Royal Knights of the classic Digital World.

Lore notes

the stories behind the Twelve
Twelve Under Homeros
The official Iliad page presents the Twelve as guardians gathered under Homeros, with Jupitermon at their head. Homeros keeps silent and rarely takes part in running the world; it has no fixed form, and the guardians revere it through a lyre symbol.
Rivals of the Royal Knights
Marsmon's profile credits the Olympos XII with abilities rivaling the Royal Knights, the guardian deities of the Net, while staying neutral in the incidents of the Digital World. Rivals in power, not enemies: no official text has them fight.
Gatherings at the Palace
The Guardian Palace, a solemn temple on Iliad's top layer, is also its information hub, and the Twelve hold their meetings there at regular intervals.
The War with the Titans
In ancient Iliad the Titans of Erebus invaded the other areas and fought a fierce war against the Twelve. The Titans were defeated and nearly wiped out; why they invaded is still unknown.
Plutomon Stands Apart
Plutomon rules Iliad's Dark Area and acts separately from the Twelve. It shares Jupitermon's goal of punishing evil, but the two have a longstanding animosity: Jupitermon does not approve of Plutomon's cruelty.
Forms, Not Extra Seats
Several Digimon tied to the Twelve are forms of a member, not new members: Mervamon is Minervamon after amassing experience, Ceresmon Medium is Ceresmon's true body riding on its head, and GraceNovamon is the fusion of Apollomon and Dianamon.
Merukimon Is Not Mercurymon
Merukimon is sometimes confused with Mercurymon, Frontier's Legendary Warrior of Steel (Mercuremon in Japanese). The official English Reference Book and DigimonWiki keep them as two separate Digimon.
