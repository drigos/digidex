Bancho Digimon

Digimon that earned the title of Bancho, the ringleader: they wear a gakuran jacket, carry unbroken pride and conviction, and every one of them reached the Mega level regardless of its original form. The title is passed on to a worthy Digimon of the next generation.

Lore notes

the stories behind the title
A Title Taken from Old Movies
The first Bancho were Digimon that watched old movies and came to admire the ringleaders they saw in them. Beyond the five members shown here, other Digimon are said to bear the title.
Born on the Accel
The group debuted in the Digimon Accel virtual pets (2006), whose storyline follows the mysterious BAN-TYO and their fight for justice against the Digimon pulling the Digital World's strings from behind the scenes.
Codename: BAN-TYO
Darkdramon's official profile tells the enemy's side, as rumor it calls unreliable: the D-Brigade lost a Tankdramon in an operation against a target codenamed BAN-TYO, recovered it and Digivolved it with a vast amount of dark matter into Darkdramon, and the berserk result fled; it is said to still hunt Codename BAN-TYO.
Chaosmon
BanchoLeomon and Darkdramon are believed to DNA Digivolve into Chaosmon, which keeps both DigiCores and is highly unstable as a result. The fusion ties BanchoLeomon to the brigade's story, not to its ranks.
Spencer's Partner
The group as such never appeared in an anime, but BanchoLeomon had a central role in Data Squad as the Partner Digimon of Spencer Damon, the protagonist's father.
