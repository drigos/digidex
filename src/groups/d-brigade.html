Special group

D-Brigade

A mechanized brigade of Dragon Cyborg Digimon: a special forces unit deployed on missions that must never become public. Who commands it, and how it is organized, remains unknown; what is known comes from its members' own profiles.

Lore notes

what the files reveal
A Brigade in the Shadows
Official material describes a mechanized special forces brigade of Dragon Cyborg Digimon, apparently focused on special operations and assassination. Digimon Profile #025 admits that its organization chart, and who commands it, are unknown.
Selection-D
A special exam screens a hundred Commandramon until only one remains; only a Commandramon that passes can Digivolve into Sealsdramon, an assassin that never relies on guns or camouflage. Many of those that fail become Hi-Commandramon instead.
Codename: BAN-TYO Unconfirmed
The brigade once ran an operation against a target codenamed BAN-TYO, and only one Tankdramon has reportedly ever been lost in the field. Rumor says researchers recovered it and Digivolved it with a vast amount of dark matter into Darkdramon; the official profile itself calls this information unreliable.
The Weapon That Fled
Darkdramon is presumed to be the brigade's ultimate weapon, but it went berserk after Digivolving and its whereabouts are unknown, even to the D-Brigade. Rumor has it that it is still searching for Codename BAN-TYO.
Brigadramon, the Lesson Learned
After failing to produce a single supremely powerful Digimon to represent the organization, the brigade developed Brigadramon, a command machine that manages attack units from the air. Three are reported in service. It commands units in the field; nothing says it leads the brigade.
Chaosmon
Chaosmon is believed to be the DNA Digivolution of BanchoLeomon and Darkdramon. Unlike a normal Jogress it keeps both DigiCores, which leaves it highly unstable. BanchoLeomon belongs to the Bancho, not to the brigade.
Other Continuities Continuity-Specific
Games extend the roster: DigimonWiki and Wikimon list Armormon and Cannondramon serving the brigade in Digimon Crusader and Digimon Heroes!. Their official profiles are not part of this roster.
