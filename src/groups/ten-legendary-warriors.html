Ten Legendary Warriors

The warriors who saved the Ancient Digital World, each the master of one element and said to be among the first Mega Digimon. They belong to legendary history: a power of the distant past whose strength survives in the twenty Legendary Spirits, not an organization serving the present-day world.

Lore notes

the stories behind the ancient war
Saviors of the Ancient World Legendary
Every one of the ten Japanese Reference Book profiles opens the same way: a Legendary Warrior who saved the Ancient Digital World, and the first Mega to exist, in the distant past.
Sealing Lucemon Legendary
AncientGreymon's profile says it survived the holy war to the end alongside AncientGarurumon and is said to have sealed Lucemon. The other eight profiles do not mention Lucemon at all.
Twenty Spirits
Digimon Profile #014 describes the Spirits as items that seal the power of the Ten Legendary Warriors, two kinds for each element: a Human Spirit and a Beast Spirit, twenty in all.
History, Not Government
No official text ties the ten to Yggdrasill or the Royal Knights. They are a legend of the Ancient Digital World, not a present-day guardian order or anyone's subordinates.
Heirs in Frontier Continuity-Specific
In Frontier the warriors left their Spirits with the Celestial Digimon, and the chosen children Spirit Evolve with them. The site's Frontier selection screen follows that inheritance.
Ancestors of Species
AncientBeetlemon's legacy passed to the Insectoid Digimon, and the site's Agumon, Gabumon and Tentomon trees draw ancestral links from AncientGreymon, AncientGarurumon and AncientBeetlemon to the lines they are said to have spawned.
