Digital World (Classic)

Royal Knights

The thirteen Holy Knight Digimon gathered as the Digital World's highest form of network security. Each knight serves with its own duty and its own reading of justice, which does not always align with the others.

Lore notes

the stories behind the order
Highest Rank of Network Security
King Drasil, the host computer that rules the Digital World, assembled the thirteen knights as the highest rank of Network Security and personally grants the title.
The Thirteenth Seat
For years the order counted only twelve members for lack of a worthy candidate. Gankoomon put Hackmon through its harsh training regimen; when it finally evolved into Jesmon, the long-vacant thirteenth seat was filled and the order completed.
Two Knights Served Lucemon
In Frontier, Dynasmon and Crusadermon saw Lucemon as the only one able to bring order to a chaotic Digital World, and harvested the DigiCode of whole Areas to release it from the Dark Area. Every knight is righteous; each follows its own justice.
Equals on Another Server
Marsmon's profile credits the Olympos XII with abilities rivaling the order's. They watch over Digital World: Iliad, which a 2014 Premium Bandai text places on another server of the network, where the knights have not interfered.
The Founder's Crisis
Imperialdramon Paladin Mode founded the order as the one who saved the ancient Digital World from total collapse in a time of unprecedented crisis.
The Empty Thirteenth Screen
In the movie X-Evolution the twelve knights confer through a circle of twelve viewscreens with an empty space for a thirteenth, and Alphamon is acknowledged but said to not actually exist. Not even the knights can physically meet King Drasil.
Justice Divided in Data Squad
In Data Squad, King Drasil deems humanity unworthy and orders the knights to destroy the Real World before it drags the Digital World down with it. Their battles with DATS and the influence of Kentaurosmon make the order waver, knight by knight.
