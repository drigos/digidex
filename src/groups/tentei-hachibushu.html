Digital World: Shambala

Tentei Hachibushu

The eight Digimon summoned by Kunlun, Shambala's host computer, to protect the present world in place of the legendary Susanoomon. Shakamon speaks for Kunlun; three warlords guard Genshi Continent, three deities guard Ashino Island, and Enmamon watches over Yomi below.

Lore notes

the stories behind the eight
In Place of Susanoomon
The official Shambala page calls the Eight the Digimon summoned by Kunlun, and says they protect present-day Shambala in place of Susanoomon, the legendary being that rewrote the world. Susanoomon itself is not one of the eight.
The Voice of Heaven
Kunlun normally lies dormant. Shakamon hears its will as the voice of heaven and commands the Eight as its spokesperson; its profile adds that it sets trials for Digimon out of compassion.
Two Creeds
The Saneiketsu uphold a law where the strong devour the weak, a free-for-all of rival warlords. The Sanmyojin uphold the principle that Digimon adapt to diverse environments and evolve. Each creed matches the region its trio guards.
A Pagoda Made of Shakamon
Takutoumon's pagoda is built from Shakamon's data, and its light quells the will to fight: a warlord whose signature weapon carries the leader's own data.
Gates to Other Worlds
Kaguyamon's profile says it can open gates to distant places and even to other Digital Worlds, one of the few official hints of travel between Shambala and its neighbors.
A Ruler Bound to Yomi
Enmamon judges the DigiCores of the dead and confines bugged ones in Yomi for good, yet the same system binds Enmamon itself: it cannot ascend to the world above.
Four and Four
On the Digimon Pendulum COLOR virtual pets (2025), the Eight are split across two versions: Shakamon and the Saneiketsu in Saiyu Warriors, the Sanmyojin and Enmamon in Toho Braves.
