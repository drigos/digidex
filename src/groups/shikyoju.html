Digital World: Shambala

Shikyoju

The Four Perilous Beasts: inauspicious Digimon that have existed in the eastern Digital World since ancient times, each named after one of the Four Perils of Chinese mythology. Yellow talismans bind them to Jakeizan, a vast mountain range said to be home to many evil spirits.

Lore notes

the stories behind the seals
Four Inauspicious Beasts
The official English profiles gloss Shikyoju as four inauspicious beasts that have existed in the eastern parts of the Digital World since ancient times. In Chinese mythology the Four Perils (Hundun, Qiongqi, Taotie and Taowu) are the four fiends banished to the edges of the world.
As Old as the East
Yihumon's profile says there is a high chance it has existed since the earliest times, when the eastern regions of the Digital World emerged: the Shikyoju may be older than present-day Shambala itself.
Yellow Talismans
The yellow charms on Yihumon match those on Dijiangmon and are inscribed with the word Shikyoju, along with data on ancient seals and barriers. Taotiemon's head seals mean inescapable confinement within Jakeizan, and Taowumon wakes only to ram the barrier that binds it there.
Who Sealed Them? Unconfirmed
The talismans on Taowumon's tusks carry traces of data from a Digimon said to be the god who governs regeneration in the eastern Digital World, presumed deeply involved in the sealing. The epithet matches Susanoomon's, but no official text names it; Taotiemon's profile only speaks of an immensely powerful being.
Forces of Ruin
Each beast embodies a destructive drive: Dijiangmon robs others of life and sets them against each other, Yihumon brims with malice, Taotiemon is never sated, and Taowumon spends its waking moments trying to break free.
Mirror of the Four Sovereigns Thematic Parallel
Both groups come in fours and draw on Chinese cosmology, but they stand for opposite ideas: the Four Sovereigns guard the compass and its order, while the Shikyoju are ancient perils held under seal. No official source ties the two groups together or has them fight.
