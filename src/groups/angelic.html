Angelic Digimon

The official encyclopedia classifies its holy Digimon with the nine ranks of classical angelology, from Angel up to Seraph. This roster groups them by power: The Three Celestials guarding the Kernel, the Angelic Army that preserves the order of the Digital World, and the fallen forms the angels can become.
