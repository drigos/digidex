Angelic Digimon

The official encyclopedia classifies its holy Digimon by angelic rank, from Angel up to Seraph; the Japanese Types follow the nine ranks of classical angelology. This roster groups them by power: the Celestial Digimon guarding the Kernel, the Angelic Army that preserves the order of the Digital World, and the fallen forms the angels can become.

Lore notes

the stories behind the ranks and the falls
Roles of the Celestial Digimon
Ophanimon's official profile defines the division of the trio guarding the Kernel: Seraphimon is the unforgiving Marshall of God (the law), Cherubimon the steadfast defender of God and wisdom, and Ophanimon the merciful and compassionate side, the holy mother of the Digital World.
Ophanimon, the Fallen Goddess
Lilithmon's official profile says it is thought to have once been an Ophanimon that fell from grace, becoming the Goddess of Darkness. A perfect mirror of Seraphimon and Daemon: two of the Celestial Digimon have a Demon Lord as their fallen counterpart.
Rasielmon, a Super Angel in the Army
Rasielmon is said to have once held the same rank as Ophanimon, and both are Super Angels. It can look out on the entire world from the Kernel and has built up the knowledge of a god, yet the official poll lists it in the Angelic Army, not among the Celestial Digimon.
Dominimon, the Phantom
Dominimon does not answer in any way to the angelic chain of command: it manifests via mutation when the balance of light and darkness breaks, is almost never sighted, and only joins ClavisAngemon and SlashAngemon against extreme threats, sacrificing its own lifeforce (Devotion Field).
ClavisAngemon and the Zenith Gate
ClavisAngemon guards the Zenith Gate, sealed by 360 doors between the Digital World and the outside, holding The Key, a master key only it can wield. Some suspect the Digimon itself is part of the key, whose power could collapse the Digital World.
SlashAngemon Leads the Army
SlashAngemon's official profile confirms it commands the Angelic Army (Powers).
The War Against Millenniummon
In Digimon Adventure: (the 2020 reboot, episode 5), Seraphimon, Ophanimon, Cherubimon, ClavisAngemon, SlashAngemon, Rasielmon and Valdurmon fought Millenniummon together in the distant past.
Three Falls, Three Stories
ShadowSeraphimon is officially Seraphimon swallowed by darkness as its concealed resentment swelled, and is said to fall into an Evil King Digimon if it keeps this form too long. In Frontier, Cherubimon came to believe Seraphimon and Ophanimon served only the Human clan, and Lucemon shrouded it in an evil aura (episodes 34 and 38). Ophanimon Falldown Mode is an Ophanimon so angry it suppressed its own emotions and fell into madness; in Adventure tri. it is born from Kari's despair and fuses with Raguelmon into Ordinemon.
