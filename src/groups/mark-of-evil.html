Mark of Evil

Not an organization: a recognizable sigil, drawn as a stylized bat, worn by Digimon that possess dark powers. It is usually red, varies in size and shape, and sits in a different place on each bearer. This roster records where.

Lore notes

the stories behind the sigil
Proof of a Fall
Devimon's official profile is the only one that names the mark: Devimon is an Angemon-species Digimon that fell to the Dark Area, and the prominent Mark of Evil on its chest is proof of it.
A Sigil, Not an Order
The bearers share no leader, mission or ranks. The wikis describe the mark as a sign of dark powers that usually, though not always, marks a Digimon as evil, often with a tie to the Dark Area.
The Bat Shape
The stylized bat silhouette is how Wikimon and DigimonWiki describe the mark; no official profile describes its shape, and its size and color change from one Digimon to the next.
A Mark That Moves with the Form
Myotismon wears five marks in three colors; VenomMyotismon keeps three, MaloMyotismon a single one on the chest. Myotismon X carries it on the coat, which its profile reveals to be its real body.
Two Crowned Bearers
Among the Seven Great Demon Lords, only Lilithmon and Belphemon Rage Mode appear on the list. Lilithmon's is black, one of the few marks not drawn in red, and its X form moves it across the right eye.
Where the Wikis Disagree
Wikimon and DigimonWiki do not always agree: only Wikimon lists SkullSatamon's chest mark and Myotismon X's coat, DigimonWiki adds Sangloupmon's DigiCore, and VenomMyotismon's red mark sits on the abdomen or the groin depending on the page.
PetitMamon Continuity-Specific
PetitMamon are the devil siblings Pal and Pul from the V-Tamer 01 manga. They have no Reference Book entry, so their card shows the card artwork of both siblings, and the level follows that card.
